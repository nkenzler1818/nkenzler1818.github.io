Projects / Photography

Photography

This collection ranges from polished final edits to lighting experiments and style tests. I lost a significant portion of my early work during my junior year of college due to a backup failure, but these represent the shots I was able to keep and continue building on. I have hands-on experience across multiple software suites at every step of the process, from capture through final edit. I've worked with Photoshop, Lightroom, Premiere, Final Cut Pro, LUT packs, and more. On the hardware side, I have experience with DSLRs, mirrorless camera systems, gimbals, tripods, lighting systems, lavalier microphones, drones, and more. Photography is constantly evolving for me, and once I land my first post-college job, I'm investing in upgraded camera gear to take this further.
